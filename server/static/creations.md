# Creations classifications

This page explains the classification used by the creations dashboard and indicated by colour coding.

- `deleted` (black) means the item has been deleted.
- `redirect` (purple) means the item is a redirect.
- `empty` (white) means overall notability is `none` and the item has neither sitelinks nor claims.
- `none` (red) means overall notability is `none` and the item does not meet the special `partial-*` or `empty` cases.
- `partial-weak` (red and orange stripes) means overall notability is `partial-weak`, usually because one side of N2 has only `weak` support while the other side is absent.
- `partial-strong` (red and green stripes) means overall notability is `partial-strong`, usually because one side of N2 has `strong` support while the other side is absent.
- `weak` (orange) means overall notability is `weak`.
- `strong` (green) means overall notability is `strong`.
- `unknown` (grey) means evaluation is incomplete, and none of the completed evaluations concluded `strong` overall notability.

Generally, `strong` means an item has substantial evidence that it satisfies Wikidata’s notability criteria, although that evidence still requires human evaluation. Conversely, `none` indicates that the automated evaluation found no evidence supporting notability.

In practice, administrators may choose to focus enforcement efforts on the clearest cases, particularly `empty` and `none`. The intermediate categories `partial-weak`, `partial-strong`, and `weak` contain items where some potential evidence of notability was found, but which are still unlikely to satisfy the criteria in their current state.
